Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who we are

PropFlow KE ("PropFlow", "we") provides property and rent-management software for landlords in Kenya. For the personal data of landlords who register, we act as data controller. For the tenant data a landlord enters, the landlord is the controller and PropFlow acts as their processor. We process personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

2. Data we collect

Account data: name, email, phone number, business name and profile photo. Property data: properties, houses, rooms and rents. Tenant data you enter: name, phone, email, move-in date, deposit and payment records. Payment data: amounts, dates and references (for example Paystack or M-Pesa receipt codes). We never see or store card numbers or M-Pesa PINs — those are handled by Paystack and Safaricom.

3. How we use it

To run the service: calculating monthly rent due dates, showing who has paid or is late, producing tenant statements, sending password-reset emails, and keeping your account secure. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

4. AI features

When you use Quick Confirm M-Pesa or Polish with AI, the text you submit (for example an M-Pesa confirmation SMS or a reminder draft) is sent through our servers to NVIDIA NIM to be processed, and is not used by PropFlow to train models. Avoid pasting information that is not needed. AI output is always shown to you for review before anything is saved or sent.

5. Service providers

We rely on Google Firebase (authentication, database, hosting), Paystack (card and mobile-money checkout) and NVIDIA (AI processing). These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms and may do so outside Kenya, with appropriate safeguards.

6. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete properties, tenants and maintenance records inside the app. Financial records may be retained where needed to meet legal obligations. Ask us to delete your account at the address below.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, object to certain processing, and complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke). Tenants should direct requests to their landlord, who controls their data.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to the account owner by database security rules, and sign-in uses Firebase Authentication. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: support@propflow.ke. We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
